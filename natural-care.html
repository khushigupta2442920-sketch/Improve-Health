INGREDIENT SAFETY GUIDE

Know before you try

01

Aloe vera

May feel soothing for some people, but it is not proven to remove pigmentation and can cause irritation in some users.

Patch test · Stop if irritated
02

Besan, curd & clay masks

These are not essential skin treatments. Frequent use or rubbing may dry or irritate skin, especially sensitive skin.

Use caution
03

Lemon juice & baking soda

Avoid applying these to facial skin. They can irritate skin; lemon juice can also cause a sun-related reaction.

Avoid on face
ⓘ
When to seek help

For severe acne, persistent irritation or a new, changing, bleeding or non-healing spot, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
