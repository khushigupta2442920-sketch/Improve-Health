A FEW IMPORTANT NOTES

Your wellbeing and privacy matter.

Please understand the current prototype's limits before using it for personal notes.

✳♧

How this prototype stores information

HealthNest's starter version stores goals, diary entries and preferences in your browser's local storage. The information stays on this browser profile and is not automatically backed up or synced to another device.

Use a personal device

Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view locally saved information. Avoid saving highly sensitive health details in this prototype.

Health information is educational

HealthNest does not diagnose conditions or provide personalised medical treatment. Natural products can cause irritation and are not always safe or effective. Seek qualified professional advice for ongoing or concerning symptoms.

Before a public launch

The production version should have a reviewed privacy notice, data minimisation, secure authentication, access controls, a tested export/delete process and appropriate review of applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements.
